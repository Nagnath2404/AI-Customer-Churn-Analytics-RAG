RETENTION OFFER GUIDELINES

Retention offers should be personalized according to customer risk and circumstances.

High-risk customers may be considered for:
- Suitable plan upgrades
- Contract incentives
- Service bundles
- Technical support assistance
- Personalized customer service

Offer priority should consider:
1. Churn probability
2. Contract type
3. Customer tenure
4. Monthly charges
5. Service configuration
6. Customer support requirements

Important:
Do not automatically offer a discount to every customer.

The retention team should select an appropriate action based on the customer's situation and company policy.
