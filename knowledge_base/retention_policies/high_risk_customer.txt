HIGH-RISK CUSTOMER RETENTION POLICY

Purpose:
This policy provides recommended actions for customers identified as high churn risk.

High-risk definition:
A customer is considered high risk when the predicted churn probability is 70% or higher.

Recommended actions:

1. Review the customer's contract type.
2. Review the customer's tenure.
3. Review monthly charges.
4. Check whether technical support and online security services are enabled.
5. Review the customer's payment method.
6. Contact the customer with a personalized retention offer.

Month-to-month customers:
Customers on month-to-month contracts should be encouraged to consider one-year or two-year contracts when appropriate.

New customers:
Customers with low tenure should receive additional onboarding and support because early-stage customers may require additional assistance.

High monthly charges:
Customers with relatively high monthly charges should be reviewed for suitable plans or bundles that provide better perceived value.

Technical support:
If technical support is not active, explain available support options and determine whether support services could improve the customer's experience.

Online security:
If online security is not active, explain available security features and recommend an appropriate security package when relevant.

Payment method:
If the customer uses electronic check, verify that billing is working correctly and explain available automatic payment options.

Important:
Retention actions should be personalized. Do not automatically provide discounts to every high-risk customer.
