CONTRACT UPGRADE GUIDELINES

Month-to-month customers have greater retention risk than customers on longer contracts.

Recommended approach:
- Explain the benefits of one-year and two-year contracts.
- Compare the customer's current plan with available longer-term plans.
- Consider a suitable retention incentive when company policy allows it.
- Avoid forcing a contract change.
- Confirm that the recommended plan matches the customer's needs.

Priority:
Month-to-month customers with short tenure and high monthly charges should receive higher retention attention.
