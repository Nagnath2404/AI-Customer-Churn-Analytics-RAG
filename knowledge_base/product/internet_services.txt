INTERNET SERVICE INFORMATION

Fiber optic:
- High-speed internet service.
- Suitable for customers requiring high bandwidth.
- Can be appropriate for streaming, gaming, and multiple connected devices.

DSL:
- Broadband internet service.
- Suitable for customers with moderate internet requirements.

No internet service:
- Customers without internet service may have different product requirements.
- Do not recommend an internet upgrade unless it matches the customer's needs.

Recommendation guideline:
Before recommending an internet plan, understand the customer's usage requirements and current service experience.
