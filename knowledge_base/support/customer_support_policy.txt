CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY

Customers should have access to appropriate technical support.

If Tech Support is not active:
- Explain available technical support options.
- Ask whether the customer has experienced technical problems.
- Recommend support services when appropriate.
- Prioritize customers who have repeated service difficulties.

If Online Security is not active:
- Explain available online security features.
- Recommend appropriate security services based on customer needs.

The goal is to improve customer experience rather than sell unnecessary services.
